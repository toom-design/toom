EU Pavilion Staff Uniform System

A unisex uniform designed for the European Union Pavilion at Expo 2025 Osaka — inspired by the theme “Nurturing Tomorrow” and the values of the New European Bauhaus.

Overview

The uniform was created for EU Pavilion staff at Expo 2025 Osaka. It reflects the Pavilion concept of “Nurturing Tomorrow” and the spirit of “Designing Future Society for Our Lives”.

Designed to be comfortable, functional and inclusive for all genders and body types in Osaka's warm climate.
