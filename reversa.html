Fall / Winter 2026 — Collection N°01

REVERSA

A dialogue between the past and the present.

The Concept

Renaissance silhouettes, reinterpreted with a softer, contemporary hand — a fusion, not a contrast.

The collection represents a dialogue between the past and the present, where the structured, formal silhouettes of the Renaissance are reinterpreted with a softer, contemporary touch. It is not merely a contrast of styles but a fusion of elements, blending historical influences with modern design while pushing the boundaries of my creative approach.

Kolekcia predstavuje dialóg medzi minulosťou a súčasnosťou, kde sú štruktúrované, formálne siluety renesancie reinterpretované s jemnejším, súčasným prístupom. Nie je to len kontrast štýlov, ale fúzia prvkov, spájajúca historické vplyvy s moderným dizajnom a inovatívnymi technológiami posúvajúca hranice môjho kreatívneho prístupu.

Process

From reference to form

Every look follows the same arc — a Renaissance portrait sets the silhouette, a 3D render resolves the form, a pattern translates it to cloth, and the atelier brings it to life.

LOOK 01 — Lace Bodice, Velvet Skirt

A Renaissance court portrait sets the line — high neck, structured waist — reworked here in corded lace over velvet.

LOOK 02 — Mandarin Suit, Ecru Wool

The formal doublet, reimagined as a mandarin-collared suit in ecru wool with a tonal paisley jacquard.

LOOK 03 — The Honeycomb Gown

Period boning and panelling translated into a honeycomb lattice, hand-couched in gold cord over black velvet.

LOOK 04 — Shearling Collar

A fur-trimmed mantle reduced to its essence — a single felted shearling collar in marigold wool.
