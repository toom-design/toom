Inspo

The collection explores the concept of emerging future technologies, reflecting the advancements currently unfolding. Through the utilization of color-changing fabric and a bespoke pattern-generating script, it offers a glimpse into the potential aesthetics of future fashion. Spectators may find themselves contemplating the brightness of the future with the latest advancements, including AI, inviting each individual to ponder and draw their own conclusions.

Process

Every silhouette was proven first in toile on the stand — checking balance, drape, and the placement of the reflective panels before a single piece of final fabric was cut.

Only once the fit held did the pattern move to wool, silk, and the technical weave that carries the collection's current of teal.
